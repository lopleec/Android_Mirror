Android Mirror

A native macOS app for viewing and controlling Android phones.

  1. Drag AndroidMirror.app into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. macOS 14+ is required; both Apple Silicon and Intel are supported.
  3. Enable USB debugging or Wireless debugging on the phone. Use the app's Help menu for pairing and keyboard instructions. ADB is included; no phone app or root is required.

This build is locally signed and not Apple-notarized. macOS may block its first launch. Review the publisher and source; if you choose to open it, use the app-specific Open Anyway option in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

The app is MIT licensed. Included third-party software keeps its own licenses, provided alongside the app and in its resources.

安装安卓镜像

  1. 将 AndroidMirror.app 拖入 Applications（应用程序）。
  2. 从应用程序目录打开。需要 macOS 14+，支持 Apple Silicon 与 Intel。
  3. 在手机开启 USB 调试或无线调试。配对、键盘使用说明在应用「帮助」菜单中。已包含 ADB，无需手机配套应用或 root。

此构建采用本地签名，未经过 Apple 公证，macOS 可能阻止首次打开。确认发布者和源码后，可在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」按系统提示对该应用选择「仍要打开」，不要全局关闭 Gatekeeper。

应用采用 MIT 许可证。第三方软件保留各自许可证，随安装包及应用资源提供。
